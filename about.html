I’m Samarth Patel, an M.Sc. Automation & Robotics student at TU Dortmund University. I focus on robotics software: the connections between sensing, estimation, planning, control and the platforms that run them.

My work ranges from ROS navigation and UR10 kinematics to resource-constrained embedded Linux and recoverable software updates. I enjoy the integration problems that appear between components—and the process of making those problems reproducible.

I’m based in Dortmund, Germany.
